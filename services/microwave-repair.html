Microwave Repair in Las Vegas, NV

Built-in and over-the-range microwaves: no heat, turntable faults, and door switch safety fixes.

When you search for microwave in Las Vegas, you need more than a flyer magnet—you need a technician who tests the system, names the failed part, and respects your time.

Las Vegas Appliance Repair Pros provides professional microwave repair for homes and light commercial spaces across the valley. Desert heat, hard water, and dust change failure patterns; our diagnostics account for local conditions.

Common microwave symptoms we fix

  • Won’t start, heat, cool, drain, or spin as designed
  • Error codes on digital displays
  • Leaks, noise, burning smells, or repeated breaker trips
  • Performance that slowly declined after a move or remodel

Our repair process

  1. Book by phone with brand, model if available, and symptoms.
  2. On-site testing with meters, gauges, and model literature.
  3. Written options: repair, defer, or replace when numbers say so.
  4. Quality parts and a retest under load before we leave.
