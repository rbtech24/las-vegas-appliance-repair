How our light commercial repair visit works

Commercial-duty parts when available; clear scope limits if the unit needs a specialist restaurant house.

  1. Call with brand, model (if available), neighborhood, and symptoms.
  2. On-site testing with meters, model literature, and safety checks.
  3. Clear options: repair now, order parts, or replace when numbers say so.
  4. Retest under load before we leave whenever parts are installed.

Repair vs replace for commercial appliance appliances

We run the numbers with you: age of the unit, cost of parts, likelihood of cascading failures, and what a comparable replacement costs installed in Las Vegas. Mid-tier freestanding machines sometimes lose to replacement; built-ins, pro ranges, and premium French-door systems often win on repair. You get the math before we order expensive modules.
