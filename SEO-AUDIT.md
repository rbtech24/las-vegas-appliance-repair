### 4. Content quality & uniqueness

Programmatic local pages (areas/brands) are efficient for coverage but **Google discounts near-duplicate doorways**. Each area should include unique: neighborhoods landmarks, common housing stock notes, travel-time/route reality, and distinct FAQs. Brand pages need brand-specific failure modes (e.g. Samsung ice makers, LG linear compressors). Blogs should not be the same article with only the city name swapped — audit pairs with high Jaccard similarity.

### 5. Images & media SEO

- Heroes and cards often use **CSS `background-image`** → no crawlable `alt`, weaker Image search, harder LCP prioritization via `<img fetchpriority>`.
- Prefer real `<img>` (or `<picture>`) with descriptive alt including service + city where natural.
- Add WebP + width/height to reduce CLS.
- Logo used as favicon is OK short-term; add dedicated favicon.ico / apple-touch-icon.

### 6. Internal linking

- Global nav prevents true orphans.
- Content-body inlinks still matter for PageRank sculpting.
- Pages with weak body inlinks: **0** (see issues).
- Recommended modules: related services, nearby areas, brand×service cross-links, blog hubs by topic.

### 7. Performance & CWV (static signals only)

- Third-party Google Fonts (2 families) = extra RTT; consider self-host subset.
- Large full-bleed JPEG heroes at 1400px — compress further / responsive srcset.
- No evidence of lazy-loading strategy for below-fold card images (many are CSS backgrounds).
- Mobile sticky app bar is fine for UX; ensure it doesn't obscure content (padding-bottom present).
- **Run Lighthouse/CrUX on production domain** after deploy for real LCP/INP/CLS.
