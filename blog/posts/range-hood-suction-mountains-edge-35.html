Step-by-step checks for range hood repair

  1. Soak metal filters in degreaser.
  2. Confirm the hood is ducted, not recirculating with dead charcoal filters.
  3. Listen for motor hum without airflow.
  4. Inspect the roof/wall cap on a calm day.
  5. Upgrade undersized builders hoods when cooking style demands it.
