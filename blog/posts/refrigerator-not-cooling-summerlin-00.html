Step-by-step checks for refrigerator not cooling

  1. Confirm fridge and freezer setpoints have not been bumped after a power flicker.
  2. Feel the condenser area (usually lower rear or bottom grille) for heavy dust.
  3. Inspect door gaskets for gaps that dump cold air into a 110° kitchen.
  4. Listen for the compressor and evaporator fan during a cooling cycle.
  5. Move food to coolers if temps rise above 40°F and call for service.

FAQs

Is a warm fridge always a bad compressor?
No. Evaporator fans, dampers, defrost heaters, and control boards fail more often than sealed systems.
How fast will food spoil?
Above 40°F, perishable risk climbs quickly—especially dairy and meat. Use coolers with ice if repair is delayed.
Can dirty coils cause this in Las Vegas?
Yes. Desert dust blankets condensers and makes compressors run hot and inefficient.
