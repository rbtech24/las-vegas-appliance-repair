Step-by-step checks for refrigerator leaking water

  1. Safely power down if water nears outlets.
  2. Inspect the water filter housing for cracks after a DIY change.
  3. Check the rear water valve connection if you have an ice maker.
  4. Look for ice buildup inside the freezer back wall (blocked drain).
  5. Wipe standing water and improve airflow until a tech clears the drain.
