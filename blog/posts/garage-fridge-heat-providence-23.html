Repair versus replace is a math problem, not a sales script. Age, prior failures, energy use, and the cost of the failed system all factor in. A single failed door lock on a five-year-old washer is almost always a repair. A rusted, leaking cabinet with a dead board on a fourteen-year-old unit may not be. We will show you the numbers for Las Vegas pricing reality, including delivery and haul-away if replacement is smarter.

Maintenance still prevents emergencies. Clean dryer vents yearly, vacuum refrigerator condensers, replace water filters on schedule, and leave front-load washer doors ajar between cycles. Those habits will not fix a failed compressor, but they stop many “no heat” and “warm fridge” calls we see after dusty weeks.
